# Changelog

All notable changes to GRASP. This project follows [Semantic Versioning](https://semver.org/).

## [1.0.0] - 2026-10-12

### Added
- `SKILL.md` — the GRASP loop: **G**ather, **R**ead, **A**sk, **S**tate, **P**roceed, with per-step completion criteria and the doctrine (assume cooperation; adopt their vantage point; state, don't silently guess; defaults on; own the burden; cheap repair beats perfect first drafts).
- `references/signals.md` — catalog of ten signal shapes: vibe, trail-off, analogy, pointed finger, polite ask, fragment pile, wish-list, silence gap, frustration, excitement.
- `references/research.md` — the research base: Grice's cooperative principle and implicature, vague language (Channell), common ground (Stalnaker/Clark), deixis (Fillmore/Levinson), conversational repair (Schegloff et al.), novice-prompting HCI ("Why Johnny Can't Prompt", CHI 2023), Anthropic agent/prompting guidance, and the prompt-engineering literature.
- `examples.md` — one worked before/after case per signal shape.
